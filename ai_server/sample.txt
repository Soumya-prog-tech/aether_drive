MARS COLONY ALPHA - OPERATIONS MANUAL v4.2
CONFIDENTIAL - AUTHORIZED PERSONNEL ONLY

SECTION 1: MISSION OVERVIEW
The Mars Colony Alpha (MCA) is humanity's first permanent settlement on the Red Planet. Located in the Jezero Crater, the primary objective of MCA is to establish a self-sustaining biosphere and conduct astrobiological research. The mission is funded by the United Earth Coalition (UEC) and currently hosts a population of 150 specialists. The mission commander is Dr. Elena Vance.

SECTION 2: ENVIRONMENTAL SYSTEMS & LIFE SUPPORT
2.1 Oxygen Generation
Oxygen is generated primarily through MOXIE-IV units, which convert atmospheric CO2 into O2.
- Normal operating range: 21.5% O2 concentration.
- Warning Threshold: Below 19.5% triggers a Yellow Alert.
- Critical Threshold: Below 18.0% triggers an automated habitat lockdown.
- Backup Systems: Algae Bioreactors located in Sector 4 provide emergency oxygen for up to 72 hours.

2.2 Water Recycling
The Water Reclamation System (WRS) operates at 98.5% efficiency.
- Daily Allowance: Each crew member is allocated 15 liters of water per day for consumption and hygiene.
- Agriculture Usage: Hydroponics bays receive 45% of total recycled water.
- Maintenance: WRS filters must be changed every 30 Sols.

SECTION 3: DAILY SCHEDULE (STANDARD MARTIAN TIME)
The colony operates on a 24-hour 39-minute Sol cycle.
- 0600 - 0700: Wake up / Morning Hygiene
- 0700 - 0800: Communal Breakfast (Sector 2 Mess Hall)
- 0800 - 1200: Work Block Alpha (EVA Operations / Lab Work)
- 1200 - 1300: Lunch / System Diagnostics Review
- 1300 - 1700: Work Block Beta (Maintenance / Research)
- 1700 - 1900: Mandatory Physical Exercise (Gravity compensation)
- 1900 - 2000: Dinner
- 2000 - 2200: Free Time / Communication with Earth (latency 12 mins)
- 2200: Lights Out (essential personnel only)

SECTION 4: EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS
4.1 Solar Flare Events
In the event of a Class-X Solar Flare detection:
1. The AI Sentinel will sound a "Pattern Delta" alarm (three short sirens).
2. All personnel must proceed immediately to the Radiation Storm Shelter in the Underground Level (B2).
3. All non-essential electronics must be powered down to prevent EMP damage.
4. Shelter lockdown remains in effect for minimum 4 hours post-peak.

4.2 Hull Breach
If a pressure drop >5% is detected:
1. Automatic bulkheads will seal the affected sector within 3 seconds.
2. Personnel trapped in the affected sector must equip Emergency Pressure Suits (EPS) located in red cabinets.
3. Do not attempt to open bulkhead doors manually until pressure equalizes.

SECTION 6: HYDROPONICS & DIET
The colony is strictly vegetarian to maximize energy conversion efficiency.
- Primary crops: Potatoes, Soybeans, Spinach, and Spirulina.
- Protein source: Lab-grown cultured tissue is served on Sundays only.
- Prohibited items: Personal plant cultivation in living quarters is strictly forbidden due to cross-contamination risks (Regulation 7.B).

SECTION 7: ROVER OPERATIONS
MCA maintains a fleet of 5 pressurized rovers.
- Rover 1 (The Beagle): Long-range exploration (Range: 500km).
- Rover 2 (The Hopper): Rapid transport (Speed: 60km/h).
- Rovers 3-5: Heavy cargo transport.
- Charging: Rovers must be docked by 1800 hours daily for solar array recharging.
- Licensing: Only personnel with Class-A EVA certification may operate Rovers 1 and 2.

SECTION 8: NETWORK & DATA SECURITY
All internal data is encrypted using AES-4096.
- The "Cortex" AI mainframe is located in the Server Room (Sector 1).
- Physical access to the Server Room requires dual-key authentication from the Commander and IT Admin.
- Unauthorized attempts to bypass the firewall will result in immediate suspension of Earth communication privileges.
